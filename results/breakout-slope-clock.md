# Optimal rollout by slope and putt direction (Stimp 10)

hole putting downhill, 6 = below putting uphill, 3 = sidehill (9 o'clock is
its mirror). Slope shown as % grade (1° = 1.7%, 2° = 3.5%, 3° = 5.2%).
Green speed moves these by at most one 4-inch grid step (see findings-phase1.md).
