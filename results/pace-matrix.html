How hard to hit it: optimal pace by slope and putt direction

Each cell is the target rollout — how far past the hole the perfectly-struck putt should finish — that minimizes expected putts to hole out. die means aim to have the ball die at the front edge. Small figures are first-putt make% and 3-putt% at that target. Hover or focus a cell for the full detail.

  • green speed: Stimp 10 (8–12 shifts any cell ≤ one 4 in step)
  • 12 o'clock = ball above hole, putting downhill · 6 = uphill · 3 = sidehill (9 mirrors it)

Slope in % grade (1° = 1.7%, 2° = 3.5%, 3° = 5.2%). Flat greens have no direction, so only one cell. Physics: Penner (2002) rolling model, Holmes/Penner capture; error models calibrated to Bansal & Broadie ShotLink data and published make-% tables.
