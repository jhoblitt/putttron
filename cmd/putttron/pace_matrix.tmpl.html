How hard to hit it: optimal pace by slope and putt direction

Each cell is the target rollout — how far past the hole the perfectly-struck putt should finish — that minimizes expected putts to hole out. die means aim to have the ball die at the front edge. Small figures are first-putt make% and 3-putt% at that target. Hover or focus a cell for the full detail — click it for a map of where that cell's missed putts finished.

Slope is % grade (rise over run). Optima are sub-grid refined by a parabola through the swept 4-in rollout grid; the tooltip's plateau range is the honest uncertainty (paired-sample SE under common random numbers). Flat greens have no direction, so only one cell. Physics: Penner (2002) rolling model, Holmes/Penner capture; error models calibrated to Bansal & Broadie ShotLink data and published make-% tables. Two modeling caveats: optima are conditional on a fixed follow-up lag policy (0.25 m, sensitivity-checked in the findings), and direction error is calibrated on flat greens — steep-slope cells assume read error does not grow with break. Clicking a cell opens the dispersion map for that putt: every dot is where one simulated miss came to rest, re-simulated from the sweep's own random numbers, so the map's holed count is the same trial set behind the make% on the cell.
